A Taste of Royalty in Every Dish

Welcome to The Royal Place — Chili's Bar & Grill, where we blend the warmth of Nigerian hospitality with world-class culinary excellence. Nestled in the heart of New Owerri, our restaurant offers an unforgettable dining experience.

From sizzling grilled specialties to our signature chili-infused creations, every dish is crafted with passion and the finest ingredients. Whether you're here for a casual lunch, a romantic dinner, or a celebration with friends, we promise an experience fit for royalty.
